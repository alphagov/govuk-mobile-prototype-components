## Where it comes from

The future driving designs in Figma, which we have only as a screenshot for now. Its sizes and spacing are read from that, so they’re approximate, and will be checked against the Figma file once we have it.

The designs show only light mode. Its colours are strong, as a number plate’s are, so they’re the same in dark mode, with black text.

## Known differences from the designs

- The designs bold only the first word of a line such as “**Expired** 28 March 2026”. Text can’t yet bold part of a line, so the example bolds it all.
