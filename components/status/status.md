## Where it comes from

The future driving designs in Figma, which we have only as a screenshot for now. Its sizes and spacing are read from that, so they’re approximate, and will be checked against the Figma file once we have it. The app today shows tax and MOT differently, with `ValidityStatusView`.

## Known differences from the designs

- The designs show only light mode, so the dark mode colours are ours: the primary button’s green, and the app’s dark divider colour for the ring’s track.
