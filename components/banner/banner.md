## Proposed: the warning

The yellow `warning` comes from the future driving designs, not the app, which we have only as a screenshot for now. It may change, or never ship, and its sizes are approximate. Its yellow is GovKit’s `yellowTint80`, as in the designs. They show only light mode, so in dark mode it’s the app’s own dark pair for that yellow, from its state label: dark blue, with yellow text.

The designs’ icon is a steering wheel with a warning sign, which Material Symbols doesn’t have; `warning` is the closest.
