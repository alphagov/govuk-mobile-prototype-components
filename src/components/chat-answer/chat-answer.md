## Known differences from the app

- The app draws an answer from the Markdown GOV.UK Chat sends it. Here it’s written with the library’s text, heading, bulleted list and link, so it can hold only what they draw. The app’s answers can have numbered lists; these can’t.
- What’s in it is 16pt apart. The app’s Markdown theme spaces headings and list points its own way, so a long answer may sit a little differently.
- Its icons are Material Symbols’ error and expand_more, in place of SF’s exclamationmark.circle.fill and chevron.down, which may only be used on Apple’s platforms.
