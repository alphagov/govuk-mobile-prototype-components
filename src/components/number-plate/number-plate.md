## The typeface

Plates are drawn in DVLA-VRM, the app’s own typeface, copied unchanged from the iOS app. It’s copyright Myles Stevens and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, with “DVLA - VRM” as a Reserved Font Name. The licence is in `DVLA-VRM-OFL.txt`, beside the font, and must stay with it. It allows the font to be shared with software, but not changed and keep its name, so it isn’t converted or subsetted.
