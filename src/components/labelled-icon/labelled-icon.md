## Icons

The app’s icons are SF Symbols, which may only be used on Apple’s platforms, so it draws [Material Symbols](https://fonts.google.com/icons) instead. Give it any symbol’s name as its `icon`. The closest to the app’s are `calendar_month` for a year, `local_gas_station` for fuel and `palette` for colour.

## Known differences from the app

- Material’s symbols sit higher on their line than SF’s, so a stacked labelled icon is a few points shorter than the app’s, and its labels line up across columns, where the app’s sit lower under taller symbols.
- The app switches from stacked to inline by itself when its items don’t fit side by side. Here, choose the layout.
